Idlimbu

Medicinal Plant

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Citrus medica var. limonum ( Risso Brandis)

Common Name: Idlimbu / Rough Lemon / Citron

Family: Rutaceae

Description

A small citrus tree with green leaves and sour yellow fruits.

Medicinal Uses

  • Traditionally used to support digestion and appetite.
  • Traditionally used for stomach discomfort.
  • Traditionally used for cough and respiratory complaints.
